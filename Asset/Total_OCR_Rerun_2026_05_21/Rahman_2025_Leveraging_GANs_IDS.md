3. Proposed Method This research proposes a hybrid methodology to improve intrusion detection perfor- mnance by addressing the challenges posed by imbalanced cybersecurity datasets. The ap- proach integrates Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for synthetic data generation with Random Forest (RF) as the primary classification model GANs are employed to create synthetic attack samples, which are then combined with real data to improve class balance This technique reduces data loss and overfitting risks, ensuring better generalization across different attack types: Figure 2, intfoduced in Section 2.2,presents the general architecture of the GAN model  while Figure 3 illustrates the overall workflow of the Proposed hrbrid training process In this approach, GAN-generated synthetic data is integrated into the RF training pipeline; directly comparing RF trained on real data and RF trained with augmented data to measure the impact of GAN-based oversampling: The proposed GAN architecture consists of a generator and a discriminator: The generator comprises_ fully connected layers with LeakyReLU activation, generating synthetic attack samples that mimic real-world scenarios The discriminator classifies Ieal versus synthetic data using binary Cross- -entropy loss, ensuring that only high-quality synthetic samples contribute to dataset augmentation Both networks are trained using the Adam optimizer (learning rate = 0. .0002, 81=0.5) for stable convergence: The GAN model undergoes 10,000 epochs of training with a batch size of 64, ensuring the generation of reliable synthetic samples: The final dataset is normalized using min-max scaling to balance feature distributions. The dataset is then split into 70%/ training and 30% testing subsets to optimize the clas- sification process. Random Forest (RF) is selected as the primary classifier to assess the impact of synthetic data augmentation: The RF model is configured with 100 estimators, providing a robust and efficient baseline for evaluating the effectiveness of GAN-augmented data RF was chosen due to its ability to handle high-dimensional data, its resilience against class imbalance; and its efficiency compared to deep iearning methods_ Through extensive analysis of cybersecurity datasets, this approach ensures a more bal- anced data distribution by integrating GAN-generated synthetic samples into the training pipeline: The adversarial learning framework in GANs enhances data diversity producing more realistic network traffic patterns This augmentation strategy significantly improves da- taset quality, optimizing feature representations before classification: Integrating GANs with RF introduces a scalable and practical solution for handling im- balanced datasets, particularly in intrusion detection systems By ensuring that minority attack classes receive adequate representation; the proposed approach enhances dataset prepro- cessing strategies fof cybersecurity applications.
